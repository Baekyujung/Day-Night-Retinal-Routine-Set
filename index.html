☀ Day routine · hydrate, even tone, protect

A morning routine that strengthens the barrier, evens out skin tone, and shields skin from UV.

1Barrier Toner

5 Ceramides + Aquatide Peptide strengthen the skin barrier while hydrating and soothing, so skin is ready for the steps that follow.

2TXA Cream

5% Niacinamide + 5% Tranexamic Acid + Melazero V2 target dark spots and the appearance of pores for a more even skin tone.

3TXA Sunscreen

Protects from UV rays, while 5% Niacinamide + 1% TXA + 1% Panthenol keep targeting dark spots.

☾ Night routine · prep, retinal care, firm

A night routine that preps the barrier first, then delivers retinal care for firmer-looking skin.

1Barrier Toner

5 Ceramides + Aquatide Peptide support the skin barrier and help reduce irritation from retinal, prepping skin for the retinal steps.

2Retinal Dual Cream

0.05% Retinal + 5% Panthenol + 0.1% Guaiazulene + Matrixyl®3000 3% firm, soothe, and hydrate.

3Retinal Shot Booster

0.1% Retinal + Matrixyl®3000 3% + Micro-Needles target wrinkles, pores, and skin texture.
